ATOM-Pi build checklist

Prices checked August 20, 2026. A memory/NAND shortage has pushed this build up sharply: the Pi 5 16GB went from $120 to ~$205, and 2TB NVMe drives that were ~$80 two years ago are now ~$245–280. Verify every price at click-time — this category moves weekly.

Two ways to cut the cost meaningfully

  • 8GB Pi 5 instead of 16GB — saves ~$60–90. Still runs Qwen3-4B; 16GB is comfort, not necessity.
  • 1TB NVMe instead of 2TB — saves ~$100. Models and OS need well under 500GB, since the knowledge library lives on the external USB drive anyway.

Totals use the midpoint of each price range, so the estimate is a guide, not a quote. Rough total: ~$800–900.
